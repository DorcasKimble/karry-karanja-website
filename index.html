Commercial Alignment

Partnership Philosophy

Partnerships must support the educational mission, provide genuine value to the audience, and fit within the five-part wealth journey.

What I Prioritize

  • Research-driven financial services and software
  • High-yield savings and low-fee investment platforms
  • Products supporting long-term financial well-being
  • Brands with transparent, ethical terms

What I Do Not Promote

  • Get-rich-quick or speculative schemes
  • Predatory lending and payday loans
  • Gambling or sports-betting products
  • Products encouraging unnecessary debt
